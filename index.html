The project

A white-box research project for reading reality

Computational Ontology develops formal, reproducible architectures for the ontological systematization of social, real and ideal objects. It reads social phenomena as complex systems — with a shared conceptual apparatus, two validated diagnostic systems, and a public platform where they converge.

Diagnostic systems

Two validated readings, one method

Each system applies the apparatus to a concrete region, producing a reproducible structural signature rather than a black-box score.

SOCE — the exclusion cycle

The Social-Ontological Cycle of Exclusion formalises how democracies naturalise the exclusion of the Other as a five-node recursive cycle — inscription, ideology, imagined community, obedience, alterity — with a structural signature validated across eleven historical cases and five topological families.

Descriptive · prospective (early-warning) modules

EMERGENZA — constitutional endurance

Reads constitutions as emergent, transgenerational systems (after Tiziana Andina). The Transgenerational Democracy Risk Analysis Methodology and its Transgenerational Index quantify whether a constitution endures across generations, using survival, diffusion and agent-based models on the Comparative Constitutions Project dataset.

Andina · LabOnt Turin · the “better ancestors” principle

Method & governance

Open to explore, disciplined to diagnose

The two-tier rule

Exploratory use is open. Anyone may apply the published codebook and rubrics to read cases, teach, or probe the model. Canonical diagnosis requires two trained human coders working under the δ-protocol — an exploratory reading is never a validated verdict.

White-box practice

Aligned with Horizon Europe and the EU AI Act: model-agnostic pipelines, inspectable specialization, documented provenance, and human-validated verdicts. AI proposes; humans decide.

How knowledge is produced

Scientific value, made into a workflow

Frontier science is curiosity-driven and answers to reality: a model earns its standing by surviving contact with historical evidence, not by internal elegance. That value becomes a concrete pipeline — from epistemology to tested findings.

Diamond road

Open to read & to publish

No fees to authors or readers; community-run venues. The project's default aspiration.

On this road: the open corpus pack, codebook & rubrics — CC BY, no APC.
Gold road

Version of record, open

The article is open access in the journal itself, where a venue's open option fits.

On this road: journal articles published open — e.g. Contested Liberalisms (in press).
Green road

Self-archiving

A preprint or accepted version deposited in an open repository — Zenodo · OSF.

On this road: the Founding Inscription preprint, the preregistration, and in-process preprints.
