The program

Computational Ontology builds formal, testable architectures for the ontological systematization of social, real and ideal objects. The program develops two signature-based diagnostic systems — SOCE, the Social-Ontological Cycle of Exclusion, a five-node architecture that reads how contemporary democracies naturalise the exclusion of the Other, and EMERGENZA, its companion system on constitutional endurance under emergency — whose structural signatures converge in ConstitutionsPlus, the program's public research platform. The confirmatory phase of the program is carried by TRACE, a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship proposal (HORIZON-MSCA-2026-PF), currently in preparation.

The two-tier rule

Exploratory use is open. Anyone may apply the published codebook and rubrics to read cases, teach, or probe the model. Canonical diagnosis is not: a structural signature counts as canonical only when produced by two trained human coders under the δ-protocol. AI-assisted extraction may propose values; only human coders validate them.

White-box practice

In line with Horizon Europe's trustworthy-AI requirements (transparency and explicability) and the EU AI Act: model-agnostic pipelines, inspectable specialization, documented provenance, and human-validated verdicts. AI proposes; humans decide.
